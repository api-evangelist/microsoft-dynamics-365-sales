AI agents are only as capable as the tools, skills, and information they can reach. In sales, the intelligence that drives great decisions—buying signals, conversation insights, market and account context—often lives outside the customer relationships management (CRM). Getting it into the flow of work has traditionally meant costly integrations or data-duplication projects only the largest companies could sustain.
