Microsoft Dynamics 365 Commerce provides a composable, API-first architecture that separates the experience layer from the commerce engine. New licensing offers customers more flexibility to use Dynamics 365 Commerce to build custom applications, connect third-party experiences, and support new commerce scenarios. Organizations can create experiences for their specific business requirements while using Dynamics 365 Commerce capabilities for pricing, promotions, inventory, loyalty, orders, fulfillment, and other commerce processes.
