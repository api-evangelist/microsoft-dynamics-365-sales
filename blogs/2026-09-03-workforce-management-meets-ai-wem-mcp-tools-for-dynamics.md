Workforce Engagement Management (WEM) MCP tools for Dynamics 365 Service Agent bring workforce actions into Copilot, Microsoft Teams, and Outlook. Supervisors and customer service representatives can manage time-off requests, shift swaps, and shift bids using natural language, with future capabilities planned for schedule visibility, leave balances, and clock in/out experiences.
